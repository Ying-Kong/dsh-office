# Code of Conduct

Be decent. Specifically:

- Critique code, not people. "This locator silently writes into the wrong cell"
  is useful; "you clearly don't understand OOXML" is not.
- Assume the report is about a real deadline someone is stressed about.
- No harassment, discrimination, or sustained disruption. Maintainers may lock
  or remove threads, and will say why.

Reports: open a private security advisory or email the maintainer listed in
`package.json` / `pyproject.toml`.
